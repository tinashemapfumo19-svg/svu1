# Stage 7 Published Candidate Manifests

Development capability extending registered candidate generation and `EXM-19` under D-26. This does not establish mandatory seating, attendance certification or staff-to-candidate ratios.

## Publication And Roster

Independent timetable publication now freezes a candidate manifest for every live sitting in the private staff snapshot. Ordinary rosters use confirmed course enrolments; deferred rosters use the exact assigned deferred cohort. Main-room rosters exclude separate-room candidates. Alternative rooms aggregate their assigned candidates, use the published operational instruction and retain each individual's extra minutes and end time, not the room maximum for every candidate.

The student-facing internal timetable snapshot also pins candidate student numbers for all newly published sittings, not just deferred sittings. Own timetable output still strips all candidate lists and peers' arrangements. A later confirmed enrolment cannot silently join an already published sitting: both the student timetable and staff manifest include it only after a reviewed timetable revision is republished. Self-service arrangement requests and their capability flags also require this published candidacy. Staff can prepare a newly confirmed candidate's case for independent review/republication. Existing pre-manifest publications keep their historical legacy membership behaviour until republished; their manifests fail closed rather than inventing an old roster from current enrolments.

Manifests store only explicit operational fields: course/paper, sitting revision, room identity/location, start, room duration, assigned staff IDs for internal authorization, candidate student numbers, extra minutes, individual ends and shared instructions. No registration/account IDs or private arrangement notes/evidence are copied into manifests. A deterministic SHA-256 digest binds the frozen manifest content. It is a consistency checksum, not a digital signature or institutional attendance attestation. Republishing replaces the current publication snapshot, following existing timetable behaviour, while [publication editions](EXAM_PUBLICATIONS.md) preserve original frozen sources for scoped office history. Approved archival retention, disposal and historical self-service remain separate work.

Publication shares the registration-period row lock that confirmation takes for update, before validating candidates or freezing the roster. This serializes confirmation against the complete validation/publication transaction. The guarded isolated concurrency probe holds that row for update, observes the publication's blocked query, releases the lock and verifies the completed frozen cohort. Its activity polling calls `pg_stat_clear_snapshot()` because transaction-scoped activity observations can be cached; see [PostgreSQL monitoring documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/current/monitoring-stats.html).

Approved operational instructions are deliberately shared with assigned invigilators. Reviewers must keep them non-clinical; the system does not automatically detect sensitive text mistakenly entered in that field.

## Candidate-Only Revision

`POST /api/exams/periods/{id}/refresh-candidates` lets a scoped `exams.schedule` officer prepare a changed confirmed cohort without changing paper, time, room, invigilators or sitting revision. It accepts the current published timetable `revision` and a `reason`. Registry shows added/removed counts against the frozen publication and a preparation form. Equal counts with different candidate identities still count as a change; no student identifiers are added to this summary or its audit metadata.

Refresh uses the same global resource, timetable and registration-period locks as publication, compares the fresh confirmed roster and returns the timetable to draft with a new revision under the maker. Existing independently approved arrangements keep their sitting source hash. The usual independent publisher and clash/capacity/arrangement checks still apply. Old student publication snapshots remain until republication, while current manifest access is blocked during draft. New publication freezes the cohort and notifies candidates through the existing path.

Stale or non-published revisions, no-op refreshes and any changed sitting that has already started are refused. Only existing published ordinary sittings with an explicitly frozen roster participate: legacy snapshots are not reconstructed, new sittings follow normal scheduling, and deferred cohorts still require cancel/rebook if their source changes. Refresh does not allocate seats, record attendance, amend registration or introduce a minimum-notice policy. Further confirmations before publication are reviewed in the current timetable context and captured by the publication transaction; refresh is not a separate durable roster version.

Focused unit checks cover identity differences, deduplication, legacy/deferred/cancelled exclusion, started flags, input validation, permission-specific scope, row-lock ordering and stale/draft refusal. The extended exam journey checks real no-op/stale/student denials, the preparation form, unchanged sitting records, draft manifest refusal, independent publication and late-candidate inclusion.

## Authority And Interface

| Endpoint | Access |
| --- | --- |
| `GET /api/exams/sittings/{id}/manifest` | Scoped `exams.schedule` or `exams.publish` |
| `GET /api/exams/me/sittings/{id}/manifest` | `exams.duties.self`, active staff and exact published staff/account assignment; own rooms only |

Both require the current timetable revision to be published. Draft changes block reads/downloads immediately. Republished cancellation has no manifest for the removed sitting; replacement rosters remove former invigilator access. Ordinary results-view, private-arrangements and student permissions alone do not grant office manifest access. Scope is permission-specific and includes programme and intake. Responses are `no-store`. Successful reads are audited with publication revision and digest, not roster contents or case evidence.

Registry exposes candidate manifests beside published office timetables and room manifests beside own invigilation duties. The panel lists room candidates and individual operational end times. CSV downloads re-fetch through the authorized endpoint, include publication/sitting revisions and checksum, quote CSV delimiters correctly and neutralize formula-like cells. Downloaded local files remain the recipient's responsibility; no claim of automatic local-file revocation is made.

Manifest state uses Registry's existing full cache reset on sign-out and authentication loss. Timetable mutation refreshes clear the loaded panel. Close controls and long headings remain separate on mobile; table containers can scroll horizontally, without widening the document. Optional [independently reviewed seating](EXAM_SEATING.md) adds explicitly recorded seats only when its published source matches this exact manifest/publication; a separate seating checksum and revision bind those labels. No seats or attendance states are fabricated.
