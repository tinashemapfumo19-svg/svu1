## Design Rules

- Primary blue: `#1D9BF0`; hover `#1689D9`; pressed `#1479C2`.
- Blue is for action, active state, links, progress, focus, and informational state.
- White and neutral backgrounds dominate; blue should remain restrained.
- Warm beige `#F6F1E8` is for selective supporting surfaces and empty/supporting states.
- Use top navigation on desktop, bottom navigation on mobile.
- Avoid permanent dark sidebars, dense admin layouts, heavy borders, and loud gradients.
- Use an 8px spacing system.
- Use Inter or system sans font stack.
- Prefer reusable primitives: AppShell, TopNavigation, MobileBottomNavigation, PageContainer, Hero, StatCard, CourseCard, DeadlineCard, ScheduleCard, AnnouncementCard, ProgressBar, Tabs, Badge, Button, Input, Drawer, EmptyState, Skeleton, Toast.
- The student should immediately understand: what to learn next, where they stopped, what is due, what happens today, how they are progressing, and where to get help.
