# Stage 3 Course Registration

## Delivered Scope

The development pilot now supports curriculum-pinned registration periods, resumable student course selection, reasoned academic/financial clearance, and atomic course enrolment with a retained confirmation receipt. This is not a production registry sign-off or an integrated finance/results service.

This extends REG-03 and REG-09 with registration stages, REG-11 with period/course records, and REG-15 with explainable eligibility checks. [Academic decisions](ACADEMIC_PROGRESS.md) supply policy-backed progression/repeat approval foundations for REG-14/REG-16/REG-17. [Effective study-year tracking](STUDY_YEAR.md) reads confirmed receipts rather than intake arithmetic or unconsumed approvals. [Governed exceptions](EXCEPTIONS.md) add independently approved credit-load and exact timetable-pair decisions. [Programme lifecycle](LIFECYCLE.md) governs suspension/interruption/withdrawal/re-entry and invalidates draft reviews. Wider REG-12 overrides remain separate work.

## Period Setup

1. Create and publish a semester/term using the existing academic-calendar API, with a registration date window. Academic periods cannot precede the student's intake entry date.
2. In `#registry`, select a student programme and create an intake-wide registration period using that programme's pinned curriculum version, year, term, and half-credit limit.
3. Configure every offered course as scheduled or asynchronous. Scheduled courses need valid weekday/minute-of-day meetings without overlapping slots within the same course. Asynchronous courses have no meetings. Unknown delivery never silently passes eligibility.
4. Open registration. Eligible programmes in that intake and pinned curriculum see the period; wrong-curriculum period reads/writes are rejected. Migration 034 allows distinct periods for different curricula in the same intake/semester, without altering existing offerings. Published/active academic calendar status and the current UTC registration window are checked again on every save and confirmation.
5. An open period may return to planning to correct schedules only before any confirmed registration exists. Closed periods cannot reopen or change configuration. Calendar closure independently blocks new registration.

## Selection And Clearance

- The student selects courses in `#apply` after accepted-offer conversion and approved onboarding. Saving requires the current revision; stale edits return `409`.
- The engine requires all offered core courses not covered by valid [recognized credit](CREDIT_RECOGNITION.md), unique in-curriculum selections, valid course status/effective dates, credits within the configured limit, no cross-course timetable clash, and no duplicate enrolment in the same academic period. Approved recognized courses cannot be re-enrolled through ordinary selection.
- Prerequisites require published passing results from completed prior periods or independently awarded prior credit permitted by the attached policy. Recognition cannot replace an attempted course's missing or failed result. Prior enrolment requires a current independent repeat approval; higher-year registration requires policy-backed progression approval. Unknown, failed, stale, or incompatible evidence fails closed. A generic academic clearance cannot bypass these checks.
- Both academic and financial clearances are required. Each is an append-only eligible/blocked decision with a reason, evidence reference, expiry date, actor, and exact selection revision. Expired, missing, blocked, or old-revision checks fail closed.
- Staff must submit the revision they reviewed; a selection change during review returns `409` rather than clearing a different selection. Any save creates a new revision, requiring fresh clearance decisions.
- Registrar authority grants academic clearance, not financial clearance. A scoped `finance_officer` role grants registry viewing and financial clearance only. System administrators can exercise both authorities for development; this is permission separation, not a two-person approval policy.
- These are explicit manual evidence-backed clearances. They are not proof of payment generated by the pilot waiver, and do not read a finance ledger, verify balances, or calculate progression.
- Credit overloads and specific meeting-clash pairs may be resolved only through [reasoned independent exceptions](EXCEPTIONS.md), bound to current evidence/revision and expiry. They do not waive financial/academic clearance, unknown prerequisites, or progression/repeat approvals. Confirmed receipts retain applied approvals; pre-confirmation revocation restores the blocker.

## Confirmation

Confirmation is owner-only and re-evaluates all eligibility facts inside a transaction. Programme/period/calendar locks, course read locks, and a student/academic-period advisory lock prevent concurrent edits and duplicate enrolment. Database uniqueness is the final guard.

The transaction records one `REG-<academic year>-<sequence>` receipt, course enrolment rows, course/delivery/credit and clearance snapshots, academic policy/source/approval evidence, calendar dates, and one confirmation audit event. Only recognized credit actually used for core, prerequisite, or progression requirements is retained as receipt evidence and protected against ordinary revocation. Concurrent retries return the same receipt. Confirmed selections and clearances cannot be edited. Receipt retries still return the original snapshot after closure; later master-data changes do not rewrite the receipt.

The student programme remains `onboarded`; per-period registration is a separate record rather than an overwrite of historic programme state. [Programme transfers](PROGRAMME_TRANSFERS.md) retain confirmed source receipts, invalidate source drafts, and create a fresh linked destination. Destination enrolment cannot precede transfer approval; no source clearance, exception, academic approval, mark, or credit award is copied. Teaching/class allocation, financial invoicing, and progression are not created by this operation.

## API

All staff operations enforce programme/intake scope using only roles granting the authorizing permission. Self-service checks ownership independently of role scope.

| Endpoint | Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET /api/registry/me/programmes/{id}/registrations` | `registry.self` | Own periods, offerings, saved selection, latest checks, and explained blockers. |
| `PUT /api/registry/me/programmes/{id}/registrations/{periodId}` | `registry.self` | Save `revision` and unique `courseIds`. |
| `POST /api/registry/me/programmes/{id}/registrations/{periodId}/confirm` | `registry.self` | Confirm the saved `revision`; repeat safely. |
| `GET /api/registry/programmes/{id}/registrations` | `registry.view` | Scoped registration context and action capabilities. |
| `POST /api/registry/programmes/{id}/registration-periods` | `registry.registration.configure` | Create with `academicPeriodId`, `yearOfStudy`, `term`, `creditLimit`, `reason`. |
| `POST /api/registry/programmes/{id}/registration-periods/{periodId}` | `registry.registration.configure` | Configure `offerings` while planning or transition with `toStatus` and `reason`. |
| `POST /api/registry/programmes/{id}/registrations/{periodId}/checks` | `registry.registration.academic` or `.financial` | Record `revision`, `checkType`, `outcome`, `reason`, `evidenceRef`, `expiresOn`. |

Offerings use `{courseId, delivery, meetings: [{day, start, end}]}`. Weekdays are 1-7 (Monday-Sunday); times are integer minutes from midnight, with end 1440 allowed. The registrar UI supports multiple meetings, numeric credit limits, governed mode/day/outcome menus, and date/time controls.

Clearance history is retained in `registration_checks`; the UI/API show the latest decision per type. Audit events retain who changed a selection, configured a period, recorded a check, and confirmed enrolment.

## Remaining Gates

The [results pilot](RESULTS.md) provides reconciled import, independent review, and controlled publication. [Academic decisions](ACADEMIC_PROGRESS.md) connect published results to prerequisites and governed progression/repeats; [exceptions](EXCEPTIONS.md) govern credit-load and specific timetable clashes. [Lifecycle changes](LIFECYCLE.md) block new registration while inactive and require fresh draft reviews after re-entry. [Credit recognition](CREDIT_RECOGNITION.md) adds one-to-one internal/external transfers and exemptions without invented GPA. The [programme/intake/curriculum transfer pilot](PROGRAMME_TRANSFERS.md) preserves confirmed source records, enforces destination chronology, and requires fresh destination clearance; its contract records the latest verification. Approved institutional and transfer policy cases, approved study-year corrections and legacy reconciliation, many-to-many credit allocation, wider exception types, course withdrawal/access and completion, approved identity corrections, real finance eligibility/invoicing, migration reconciliation, wider accessibility/browser QA, and registry-owner sign-off remain open.
