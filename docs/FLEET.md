# Native Fleet

## Scope

The user delegated workflow selection on 2026-10-10. The replacement will support vehicle and driver registers, independent trip approval, dispatch and return, fuel receipts, servicing/safety holds and incident records. This settles the build scope, not institutional driving, accounting, privacy or retention policy.

The supplied legacy tree establishes shared fleet menu routes only. No operational source or authoritative fleet specification has been recovered. This implementation is a native replacement, not demonstrated legacy behavioural parity. No archived PHP/SQL is executed, repaired or imported.

## Storage Foundation

Migration `098_fleet_storage.sql` adds ten retained tables. It does not add fleet permissions, appointments, public routes or a fleet screen, and seeds no vehicles, drivers, authorisations or institutional evidence.

- Vehicle definitions link one-to-one to institution assets. Opening odometer and `km`/`mi` are explicit, immutable facts.
- Driver authorisations link active staff with a login account, explicit validity and evidence reference. Requester, driver and approver cannot establish self-approval; revocations are retained separately. No licence identifiers/documents or statutory renewal periods are invented.
- Trip requests are immutable. Revision-bound events permit request to approved/rejected/cancelled, approved to dispatched/cancelled, dispatched to returned. Closure is permanent. Approval is independent of requester and driver.
- Approved planned intervals are half-open; overlap checks consider both vehicle and staff identity across authorisation records. A fleet-wide transaction lock serializes operational inserts. Dispatch additionally refuses resources still dispatched, including overdue trips.
- Dispatch checks current staff/account status, revocation/validity, linked asset condition/status and current service hold. Linked shared row locks make those checks linear with status changes; they do not establish institution-wide authority. Dispatch occurs within the planned interval; actual events are finite, not future dated and do not precede request creation/previous return.
- Dispatch/return mileage cannot decrease or fall below the opening reading. Distance units never silently change. Meter replacement/reset is not supported or inferred.
- A safety hold survives servicing; release needs independent evidence from a different actor. Return remains recordable after a hold or driver revocation, without restoring availability or authority.
- Fuel receipts retain institution-unique references, positive exact millilitres, exact paid minor units, active currency, purchase date and optional accounting reference. No accounting journal is posted. Corrections/reversals and supplier-specific receipt mappings remain to build.
- Incidents bind an optional trip to its vehicle; bodies and resolutions are opaque encrypted-envelope fields. Direct storage fixtures do not prove cryptographic binding, staff privacy authority, investigation or resolution policy. An incident record alone does not silently hold/release a vehicle.
- Every table refuses update/delete. Actor-bound command-receipt storage is provided, but no public command yet couples business changes with audit/receipt or enforces replay permissions.

Service revisions are assigned under the fleet lock rather than relying on identity allocation order. Approval locks and retained revisions prevent a second concurrent overlapping approval from succeeding after the first commits. The original storage-only stage did not interlock asset disposal; that integration is now implemented below.

## Authenticated Commands

Migration 099 adds immutable driver requests/independent decisions and fuel reversals. Native roles are `fleet_officer` (operational), `fleet_approver` (independent review) and `fleet_safety_officer` (restricted incidents). All have bounded institution-wide operational viewing and existing human self-service permissions; none receive another fleet duty implicitly. Staff invitations can grant these explicit roles through the existing acceptance workflow. No real appointment is seeded. System administrators have native fleet permissions, but cannot approve their own driver requests/trips or release their own safety holds.

`server/modules/fleet.js` rechecks active accounts, active roles, current unended global assignments and the requested permission inside each transaction, including replay. Matching accounts/assignments/roles/permission rows are shared-locked while the operation is in flight. An unrelated global role cannot widen a scoped fleet permission. Read snapshots fail closed with a refresh conflict if authority changes concurrently.

Commands are actor-bound and HMAC-keyed, reject unknown/spoofed fields, use canonical exact quantity strings and strict real UTC timestamps, and atomically couple business changes, metadata-only audit and retained receipt. Replays return only recorded IDs/revisions and require current authority. Changed input under a reused key conflicts. No private incident detail, policy body or resolution text is placed in audit metadata or command results. Key unavailability prevents commands and private incident reads.

Public routes (all responses `no-store`, duplicate query keys refused, command query parameters refused):

- `GET /api/fleet`: native capabilities and bounded workspace totals.
- `GET /api/fleet/references`: bounded eligible unregistered asset and active staff search for fleet managers, plus up to 200 currently active/effective currencies.
- `GET /api/fleet/{vehicles,drivers,trips,fuel,service}`: 25-row operational pages with exact offsets, bounded search and optional vehicle filter.
- `POST /api/fleet/{vehicles,drivers,trips,fuel}`: register/request/record; actor identities come from the authenticated account, never supplied actor fields.
- `POST /api/fleet/drivers/:id/decision`: independent approval or rejection of an immutable request; rejection remains possible if staff are suspended. Approval still requires active staff/account and unexpired validity.
- `POST /api/fleet/authorisations/:id/revoke`: retained revocation by current fleet approvers.
- `GET|POST /api/fleet/trips/:id/events`: bounded retained history or revision-bound independent decision/dispatch/return/cancellation.
- `POST /api/fleet/vehicles/:id/service`: revision-bound hold/service by managers, independent release by approvers.
- `POST /api/fleet/fuel/:id/reverse`: independent retained reversal; the original receipt stays visible and flagged, never erased or silently journalled.
- `GET|POST /api/fleet/incidents`, `GET /api/fleet/incidents/:id`, `POST /api/fleet/incidents/:id/resolve`: separate restricted incident permission. Collection rows have metadata only. Authenticated details bind record, vehicle, trip and recorder cryptographically; resolutions bind incident and resolver. Both private texts are bounded at 2,000 characters. Caller-provided policy/retention references are encrypted context, not proof of institutional approval.

Asset commands now acquire the fleet operations lock before asset row locks. A storage trigger refuses `disposal_pending`/`disposed` transitions for vehicles with approved/dispatched trips. Native disposal refusal rolls back its proposal, audit and receipt; closing/cancelling outstanding trips permits the existing independent disposal workflow. Nonserviceable status and service holds still block dispatch; recording a return remains possible after a hold/revocation.

The Fleet staff workspace exposes vehicles, drivers, trips, fuel, service and separately restricted incidents. Registration/description corrections, meter replacement/reset and retained reading corrections with independent review are implemented below. Incident investigation/retention/export/deletion/key operations, supplier receipt mapping and statutory/accounting policy remain explicit work/gates. Fleet does not post cashbook, fuel expense or student-ledger entries.

## Meter Periods And Reading Corrections

Migration 101 adds retained meter replacement/reset proposals and independent decisions, retained opening/trip/closing reading correction proposals and independent decisions, and an explicit meter-period field on trip events. Existing events acquire period-zero metadata; no original reading, timestamp, evidence or actor is overwritten. Vehicle units and asset identity stay fixed. There are nineteen Fleet tables after this migration.

Physical readings are ordered within each meter period. Approved replacement/reset records retain the old closing reading and the new opening reading/reference/time. Cumulative readings are derived from effective period boundaries and physical readings using exact bigint arithmetic; a new meter can begin at zero or any explicit nonzero reading, without inferred unit conversion or a negative period distance. Corrections retain originals and can address historical as well as current opening, trip and closing readings. Approval rechecks neighbouring effective readings and period boundaries; it cannot create negative distance or cross the next reading.

Proposals bind the current meter revision, approved-reading revision and last current-period trip-reading identity. Independent approval rechecks that snapshot and current asset status under the fleet lock. Both proposal and approval are refused while dispatched. Stale or retired-asset proposals remain rejectable. Unresolved meter/reading reviews block dispatch until independently resolved; they do not release service holds. Existing return handling after a driver revocation or safety hold remains available. Dispatch and return bind the current meter period and actual time; omitted period metadata is compatible only with period zero, never silently accepted after replacement.

New operational routes use existing fresh global Fleet permissions, actor-bound HMAC receipts, strict fields, atomic audit/receipt writes and no-store responses:

- `GET /api/fleet/vehicles/:id/meters`: current physical/cumulative state plus independently paged change history, retained reading targets and correction history; exact `offset`, `readingOffset`, `correctionOffset` only, 25 rows per collection.
- `POST /api/fleet/vehicles/:id/meters`: manager replacement/reset proposal with exact old closing/new opening readings, meter reference/time, evidence and current snapshot.
- `POST /api/fleet/vehicles/:id/readings`: manager correction of an existing opening/trip/closing reading with exact target identity/period, evidence and current snapshot.
- `POST /api/fleet/meter-changes/:id/decision`, `POST /api/fleet/reading-corrections/:id/decision`: independent approver decisions, revision-bound and replayable only with current authority.

The Vehicles workspace includes Meter history, exact physical/cumulative labels, change/correction forms and separate pagers/review controls. Trip history presents original, effective and cumulative readings with their period. Existing in-memory drafts, frozen uncertain commands, account/route clearing and escaped rendering apply. References are evidence supplied by authorised users, not statutory calibration, driver/compliance or institutional policy approval.
